The Gap

The Hardware Exists.
The Brain Doesn't.

Humanoid hardware costs dropped below $100K. Billions are invested in bodies. But robots still fail the moment they leave the demo floor. The bottleneck has always been intelligence — and we're here to solve it.

Manufacturers have hardware — not cognitive AI
Robotics companies build impressive hardware, then discover world-class AI requires entirely different expertise. Hardware companies cannot become AI labs overnight.
Enterprises expected autonomy — got babysitters
Companies purchase robots expecting productivity gains, then hire entire teams of "robot trainers" to manually supervise every unit. The ROI never arrives.
Task-trained AI breaks outside training data
Every new object, environment, or context requires fresh training runs costing months and millions. The real world doesn't wait for retraining cycles.
